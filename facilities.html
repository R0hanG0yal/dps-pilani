Six Specialized Laboratories

Fully equipped to support foundational inquiry, senior secondary board practicals, and creative expression under expert faculty supervision.

Lab 01 · Science

Physics Laboratory

Equipped with precision optical benches, focal length apparatus, resistance coils, galvanometers, potentiometers, sonometers, and mechanics experiments aligned with CBSE practical syllabi.

Individual workstations • Board-certified
Lab 02 · Science

Chemistry Laboratory

Acid-resistant ceramic workstations, individual gas burners, titration burette stands, analytical balances, chemical store room, and safety eye-wash showers.

Safe chemical storage • Fume exhaust
Lab 03 · Life Science

Biology Laboratory

High-resolution monocular and binocular compound microscopes, human anatomical models, preserved botanical and zoological specimens, and dissection demonstration tools.

Botanical & physiological specimens
Lab 04 · Digital IT

Computer Science & IT Hub

Modern network workstations, high-speed fiber broadband, regulated operating systems, and developer environments for Python, SQL, C++, HTML5, and Scratch logic.

1:1 terminal access • UPS power redundancy
Lab 05 · Mathematics

Mathematics & Logic Lab

Concrete mathematical manipulatives, 3D geometric polyhedrons, Vedic mathematics calculation boards, abacus sets, and graphing boards that demystify abstract formulas.

Experiential geometry • Spatial reasoning
Lab 06 · Performing Arts

Music & Performing Arts Studio

Knowledge Resource Centre

Intellectual Library & Reading Rooms

A serene academic sanctuary housing over 5,000 cataloged volumes, competitive entrance archives, and national periodicals.

Curated Reading Sanctuary for Independent Inquiry

The Central Library at Durga Public School is designed to foster deep concentration, critical reading, and self-directed scholarly discovery. Open throughout school hours and supervised by a full-time librarian, the facility serves students from Class I through senior board cohorts.

  • 5,000+ Cataloged Titles: Fiction, non-fiction, encyclopedias, Hindi classics, English literature, and regional history.
  • Board & Competitive Entrance Bank: Exemplar NCERT solutions, previous 15-year CBSE question banks, and foundational reference texts for JEE, NEET, NDA, and Olympiads.
  • Periodical & Newspaper Desk: Daily subscriptions to The Hindu, The Indian Express, Rajasthan Patrika, Dainik Bhaskar, and monthly educational digests.
  • Structured Reading Timetable: Compulsory weekly library periods for all primary and middle school cohorts.

Digital Library Protocols

Borrowing Guidelines & Digital Search

Every registered student is issued an institutional borrower pass. Scholars can check out two volumes simultaneously for a 14-day cycle, encouraging consistent home reading habits and accountable stewardship of books.

Circulation Hours: Monday to Saturday, 08:30 AM – 03:30 PM
Special Guidance: Senior secondary research support for science and commerce investigatory projects.
Institutional Logistics & Student Safety

Safe GPS Transport Fleet

Reliable, safe, and punctual daily transport serving Pilani town and adjacent rural and semi-urban clusters across Jhunjhunu and Bhiwani borders.

Fleet Unit 01

Live GPS Tracking & Speed Control

Every bus is integrated with real-time GPS positioning, enabling our transport desk to monitor route adherence, arrival timelines, and speed capped strictly at 40 km/h.

Fleet Unit 02

Verified Crew & Female Attendants

Experienced, police-verified commercial drivers accompanied by trained bus conductors and designated female support attendants for the safety of our youngest scholars.

Fleet Unit 03

Emergency Preparedness on Board

Equipped with certified on-board dry-chemical fire extinguishers, comprehensive first-aid medical trauma boxes, emergency exit doors, and mandatory seatbelts.
