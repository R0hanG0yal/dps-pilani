Historical Foundation

History & Educational Commitment

Durga Public School (also cataloged in official regional records as Durga Devi Public School) was founded around 2006 to provide comprehensive English-medium schooling in the educational township of Pilani, Rajasthan.

Situated along Loharu Road near the landmark Ganesh Temple, the school was planned with spacious, well-ventilated classrooms, specialized science laboratories, and an expansive athletic playground. For two decades, our faculty has delivered consistent scholastic rigor while preserving strong cultural values and personal integrity.

Our Vision

Scholastic Mastery with Moral Clarity

To develop independent, rational thinkers equipped with scientific temper, bilingual language fluency, and a strong sense of civic responsibility toward regional and national progress.

  • Comprehensive foundational literacy and numeracy
  • Hands-on laboratory experimentation and digital literacy
  • Compulsory sports and character-building activities
Our Mission

Structured Learning for Every Child

To maintain a safe, disciplined, and supportive school environment where dedicated teachers mentor students toward board examination excellence and wholesome personal development.

  • Regular diagnostic testing and parent consultation
  • Fair, merit-based admission and developmental support
  • Strict adherence to statutory safety and education norms
