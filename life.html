Four-House System Culture • Arts • Fitness
Healthy Rivalry

The House System

Every student belongs to a house — earning points in sport, scholarship and spirit through the year.

Green House

Gandhi House

Named for truth, harmony and selfless service. Craft exhibitions, environmental drives and civic projects carry the Gandhi banner through the year.

Saffron House

Vivekanand House

Named for courage, character and intellect. Science exhibitions, oratory summits and youth leadership symposiums lead its calendar.

Wine House

Raman House

Named for scientific brilliance and inquiry. Innovation challenges, mathematics olympiads and quiz championships define its pride.

Blue House

Bose House

Named for Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose. Fosters indomitable patriotism, disciplined marching drills, athletic championships and resolute leadership.

Creative Expression & Heritage

Cultural Festivals & Performing Arts

From vibrant Rajasthani folk dances to Hindi Diwas and national jubilees, our students take center stage in celebrating culture and expression.

Tradition & Folk

Rajasthani Folk Dance

Authentic Ghoomar and Kalbeliya folk presentations in traditional attire celebrating the vibrant heritage and desert melodies of Shekhawati.

Festivals

Ganesh Chaturthi Samaroh

Annual campus festival celebrating wisdom and prosperity near the historic Ganesh Temple on Loharu Road with devotional hymns and community sharing.

Literary Arts

Hindi Diwas & Sahitya Samaroh

Annual tribute to the national language featuring inter-house Kavita Sammelan (poetry recitation), essay competitions, and dramatic skits.

Annual Showcase

Annual Day Cultural Gala

The pinnacle stage event of the academic year featuring choreographies, theatrical drama, choral choirs, and felicitation of student achievers.

Music & Choral

Classical Choral & Orchestra

Group harmonium, tabla, and vocal ensembles trained in our dedicated music studio performing patriotic compositions and classical ragas.

Stage Arts

Tricolour & Heritage Dramatics

Dramatic tributes honouring India's freedom movement and regional pioneers presented by our secondary and senior scholars.

Physical Education & Athletic Prowess

Sports & Athletics Arena

Sprawling sand and turf athletic grounds supporting football, cricket nets, standard running tracks, volleyball courts, and traditional Indian contact sports.

Athletic Infrastructure Designed for Champions

At Durga Public School, physical stamina, tactical leadership, and sportsmanship are cultivated as core educational pillars. Our campus accommodates regulation-standard pitches and running tracks, coached by qualified physical education instructors.

  • Athletic Running Track & Sprints: 400m running tracks with dedicated lanes for 100m, 200m, 400m sprints and relay races.
  • Cricket Ground & Practice Nets: Natural turf pitch and heavy-duty practice nets for bowling and batting drills.
  • Volleyball & Basketball Courts: Regulation surfaced courts equipped for inter-house tournaments and district trials.
  • Kabaddi & Kho-Kho Arenas: Dedicated clay arenas fostering agility, reflex speed, and Indian traditional team sportsmanship.
  • Yoga & Calisthenics: Daily morning mass physical training (PT) sessions and yogic breathing routines for mental clarity.

Track Events

Track Sprint Championships

Annual 100m and 200m sprint heats test peak acceleration and cardiovascular conditioning among junior and senior house athletes.

Team Sports

High-intensity raiding, defensive ankle catches, and tactical team cohesion under referee oversight during winter sports week.

Honours

Sports Day Trophy Ceremony

Conferral of gold medals, rolling championship shields, and the coveted Best Athlete of the Year trophy at our podium celebration.

Moral Foundation & Daily Routine

Saraswati Vandana Assemblies

Daily spiritual invocations, thought-of-the-day discourses, national pledge, and value-based mentoring that anchor student life.

Assembly Protocol

Devotion • Duty • Perfection

Every morning at Durga Public School begins in the central quadrangle with the resonant recitation of Ya Kundendu Tushara Hara Dhavala — the traditional hymn invoking Maa Saraswati, Goddess of learning and wisdom.

  • Deep Prajwalan Ceremony: Lighting of the sacred oil lamp by dignitaries and student captains during auspicious functions.
  • Bilingual Thought of the Day: Daily philosophical reflections presented alternately in Hindi and English.
  • National Pride & Anthem: Collective recitation of the National Pledge followed by Jana Gana Mana.
  • Headlines & Vocabulary Drill: Student editors share daily news summaries and moral idioms to enrich current awareness.
Assembly Timings: Monday to Saturday, 08:00 AM – 08:30 AM
Guiding Philosophy: Developing humility, respect for elders, and a disciplined approach to every day.

Auspicious Puja & Aarti Ceremonies

Special prayers on Basant Panchami, Janmashtami, and session inaugural days instilling reverence for teacher-disciple guru-shishya parampara.

Central Assembly Hall

Spacious amphitheatre and quadrangle hall gathering the entire school community for value lectures, guest orations, and scholarship announcements.
