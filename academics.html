Classes XI & XII

Senior Secondary Academic Streams

Three comprehensive streams combining mandatory core subjects with specialized electives.

Stream 01

Science

Engineered for students pursuing engineering, medicine, software sciences, and technical research careers.

  • Compulsory: English Core
  • Physics & Practical Laboratory
  • Chemistry & Practical Laboratory
  • Mathematics / Biology
  • Electives: Computer Science / Physical Education / Hindi
Stream 02

Commerce

Structured for careers in finance, chartered accountancy, corporate management, economics, and business administration.

  • Compulsory: English Core
  • Accountancy
  • Business Studies
  • Economics
  • Electives: Applied Mathematics / Informatics / Physical Education
Stream 03

Humanities / Arts

Designed for civil services preparation, law, public policy, literature, journalism, and educational leadership.

  • Compulsory: English Core
  • History
  • Political Science
  • Geography / Economics
  • Electives: Hindi Sahitya / Physical Education
Evaluation Framework

Assessment Cadence & Academic Terms

Term Scheduling

Two Formal Terms

  • Term 1 (April–September): Periodic Test I, Mid-Term Diagnostics, Half-Yearly Evaluations
  • Term 2 (October–March): Periodic Test II, Pre-Boards for Board Classes, Annual Examinations
  • Parent consultations follow every formal assessment cycle
Promotion Norms

Clear, Published Standards

  • Attendance: Minimum 75% compulsory for final promotion eligibility
  • Threshold: Minimum 33% aggregate in each subject (theory & practical)
  • Continuous work: Homework notebooks, lab files & projects feed internal evaluation
