Parent Inquiries

Questions, Answered

Clear, transparent answers on admissions, streams, transport and safety.

What is the admission procedure and age criteria for Session 2026-27? +
Admissions for Session 2026-27 are open for Classes Nursery through Class XI. Children applying for Nursery must be 3+ years old as of 31st March 2026. Parents can register through the online enquiry form or collect the physical registration dossier at our Loharu Road secretariat. An informal readiness interaction is held for foundational students, followed by seat allocation upon verification of documents (Birth Certificate, TC, Aadhaar).
What academic streams are offered for Senior Secondary (Classes XI & XII)? +
We offer all three recognised senior secondary streams: Science (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science), Commerce (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics) and Humanities/Arts (History, Political Science, Geography, Hindi/English Literature).
Does the school provide safe bus transportation across Pilani? +
Yes. The school operates a fleet of dedicated buses covering all principal residential areas of Pilani town and adjacent rural feeder routes. Every bus carries first-aid kits, speed limiters and trained attendants for secure pick-up and drop-off.
What laboratory and digital facilities are available on campus? +
Our academic wing houses fully equipped practical laboratories for Physics, Chemistry and Biology, alongside a modern Computer Science laboratory with high-speed connectivity and interactive projection suites, supported by an extensive school library.
How does the school ensure student safety and clean drinking water? +
Safety is our highest priority. The campus maintains a gated perimeter with 24×7 security personnel, comprehensive CCTV coverage, certified fire-safety equipment, and commercial Reverse Osmosis (RO) drinking water plants tested routinely by local health authorities.
