Verification Dossier

Required Statutory Documentation

Original documents must be produced for physical verification during the admission interaction:

  • Official Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Authority or Gram Panchayat
  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) countersigned by competent education officer
  • Attested copy of previous class marks transcript / report card
  • Aadhaar card copy of the student and parents / legal guardians
  • Recent passport-sized photographs of the scholar (4 copies)
  • Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), if claiming statutory reservation status
