Institutional Data Governance Framework

Select any section below to review the statutory rights, processing grounds, and safeguards maintained by the school.

1. Data Fiduciary Identification & Statutory Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data by Durga Public School (also recognized in regional regulatory records as Durga Devi Public School), an educational institution situated near Ganesh Temple, Loharu Road, Pilani, District Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan 333031 ("School", "Data Fiduciary", "We").

The school processes personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and educational regulatory directives issued by the Department of School Education, Government of Rajasthan.

2. Processing Personal Data of Children (Section 9 DPDP Act)

In strict compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023:

  • The school processes personal data relating to enrolled students or applicant minors only upon obtaining verifiable consent from their parent or lawful guardian.
  • The school does not undertake behavioral monitoring, profiling, or targeted commercial tracking directed at children.
  • No data processing that may have a detrimental effect on the well-being of a child is undertaken or permitted on school systems.
  • Data collected for academic registers is retained only for the duration mandated by statutory board regulations and state archiving laws.
3. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We collect only data strictly necessary for fulfilling legitimate educational, pedagogical, and statutory governance duties:

  • Student Identity Data: Full legal name, date of birth, gender, photograph, previous school Transfer Certificate (TC), and statutory identification (Aadhaar/Birth Certificate).
  • Parent / Guardian Information: Full name, permanent and local residential postal address, verified mobile contact number, occupation, and email address.
  • Scholastic Performance Records: Attendance logs, periodic examination grades, board registration numbers, and co-curricular achievements.
  • Emergency Medical Information: Blood group, verified allergies or medical conditions, and emergency contact details for rapid clinical response.
4. Purpose of Processing & Absolute Non-Commercial Clause

Personal data collected through this portal or physical admission registers is processed exclusively for:

  • Processing admission inquiries, assessing grade eligibility, and completing formal enrollment
  • Maintaining mandatory scholastic registers under the Rajasthan Education Code
  • Communicating academic progress, attendance reports, fee receipts, and institutional notices
  • Complying with regulatory audits conducted by the Department of Education, Rajasthan
Absolute Prohibition of Commercial Sale:

Durga Public School never sells, rents, leases, or trades student or parent contact data to commercial third parties, coaching centers, textbook marketing syndicates, or advertising networks.

5. Rights of the Data Principal (Parents & Guardians)

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023, parents and legal guardians possess enforceable rights regarding their child's personal data:

  • Right to Summary of Data: Request an official summary of personal data held and identities of any educational authorities with whom it was shared.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: Request correction of inaccurate demographic records, address updates, or phone numbers in school databases.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of non-statutory data once the admission inquiry has concluded without enrollment.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Register complaints regarding personal data mishandling with our designated Grievance Officer.
